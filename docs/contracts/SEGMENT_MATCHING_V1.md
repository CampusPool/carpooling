## Minimal interval authority

`mobility_booking_intervals`: booking_id unsignedintprimary FK trip_passengers RESTRICT; trip_id unsignedintFK; quote_id UUIDFK; from_index/till_index unsignedtiny (stopindices,0<=from<till<stopcount); fare_minor unsignedbig; route_hash char64 (SHA256 of the exact persisted quote.snapshot text read from the DB, never a re-encoded response); match_reference_id nullableUUID; timestamps; indextripid. Identity/terms immutable. One canonical Passenger row represents one person's seat. Requested seats v1=1; reject larger groups rather than silently reserving one.

Capacity on each leg i =Trip.total_seats - COUNT canonical Passenger rows accepted(state1), whose immutable interval covers from_index<=i<till_index. An accepted legacy booking without interval conservatively occupies the entire route. Pending/waiting/rejected/cancelled rows do not reserve. Cancellations including lifecycle bulk cancel free capacity automatically through the canonical state; never maintain a second active/released flag/counter. Keep interval/history rows. Full-route seats_available is minimum leg free capacity, clamped>=0; specific matches use minimum over their interval. Unknown/corrupt interval fails closed as full-route occupancy.
