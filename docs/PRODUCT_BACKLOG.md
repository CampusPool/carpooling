## Totals and scope
- Seven epics, **28 user stories** and **nine NFR/engineering enablers**.
- **Sprint 1:** 42 functional + 19 enabler = **61 SP** (19–23 October).
- **Sprint 2:** 59 functional + 14 NFR/enabler = **73 SP** (26–30 October).
- **Unscheduled:** 5.1–5.4, retaining source estimates but excluded from sprint velocity.
- **Stretch in Sprint 2:** 3.3, 6.2, 6.3. Drop first if capacity is insufficient.
- `host` and `passenger` are student capabilities. Faculty/staff accounts and employee ride modes are outside the agreed students-only scope.
- Stories 4.5 and 7.1 share one distance/time-aware cost calculation implementation.
- Story points are estimates, not elapsed hours; **134 SP in two one-week sprints is a high-risk workload** until calibrated.

## How issues should move
`Backlog → Ready → In progress → In review → Done` (recommended GitHub Project status columns).

A story is **Done** only when the applicable acceptance checks pass, implementation is reviewed through a PR, and the evidence is linked back to the issue. Issue creation alone is not completion.
