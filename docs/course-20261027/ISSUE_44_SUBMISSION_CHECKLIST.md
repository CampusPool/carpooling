## Scope and privacy boundary

Use only synthetic actors, routes, credits and receipts in the demonstration. Do not include credentials, JWTs, provider keys, identity documents, precise personal locations, pilot database exports or raw reference captures. Timetable import and class-schedule matching are explicitly excluded. Email verification, live payment settlement, physical GPS/two-phone acceptance and OS push remain deferred or unverified.

## Reviewer sign-off

The reviewer should confirm that each claim links to a tracked artifact or a dated execution record, that partial and deferred states are visible, and that no automated test result is presented as teacher approval. The owner should obtain a real teammate review before moving the issue to review or done.
